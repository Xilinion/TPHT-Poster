# TPHT Poster — VLDB 2026

Poster for **Succinct and Fast Tiny Pointer Hash Tables** (VLDB 2026, research
paper #2575, PVLDB Volume 19).

- **Size:** A1 portrait (594 × 841 mm), as required by the VLDB 2026 poster
  instructions.
- **Build:** `make` (two `pdflatex` passes; needs `beamerposter`, `pgfplots`,
  `qrcode` — all in TeX Live).
- **Output:** `main.pdf`, print-ready at exact A1 dimensions.

The visual language (Cornell red palette, TikZ diagrams, result charts)
mirrors the conference talk in the sibling `TPHT-Slides` repository; the
numbers come from the paper in `TPHT-Paper`.

## Printing notes

- Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page") on A1 paper, portrait.
- The poster stand ID (R-number) is listed in the day's poster session at
  <https://vldb.org/2026/program.html> — search for paper #2575.
